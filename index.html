Who we are

Practical help for real needs.

Hebron Charity is the charitable department of Hebron Faith Church in Ethiopia. We run five practical projects that keep children in school, give young people skills for work and help families through hard times.

Our projects

Our five projects.

Each project meets a practical need: staying in school, eating well, learning a trade or getting through a crisis.

01

Feminine Hygiene Products

We produce reusable sanitary pads and give them free of charge to schoolgirls, helping girls stay in school with dignity.

Learn more about Feminine Hygiene Products →
02

School Feeding Program

We provide nutritious meals to schoolchildren from vulnerable families, helping them attend school regularly.

Learn more about School Feeding Program →
03

Vocational Training and Empowerment

Photography, graphic design and practical skills training that prepares young people for employment and self-employment.

Learn more about Vocational Training and Empowerment →
04

Emergency and Household Support

We help families in crisis with house-rent assistance, food supplies and essential hygiene and household items.

Learn more about Emergency and Household Support →
05

Compassionate Outreach

Free showers, personal hygiene services and breakfast for people in need, plus soap-making and candle-making training.

Learn more about Compassionate Outreach →
