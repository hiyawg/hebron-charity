Our projects

Practical love, rooted in the Word.

Each project meets a real need in our community and carries the love of Christ to the people we serve.

Project One

Result in Thanksgiving to God

“And through us your generosity will result in thanksgiving to God.”

2 Corinthians 9:11

Main activityGirls’ Menstrual Hygiene Project

  • Production of reusable sanitary pads
  • Distribution of free sanitary pads to schoolgirls
  • Sharing the love of Christ

Project Two

Pure Worship

“Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress…”

James 1:27

Main activitySchool Feeding Program

  • Providing nutritious meals to schoolchildren
  • Supporting children from vulnerable families
  • Helping children attend school regularly
  • Demonstrating God’s love through practical care
03

Project Three

Give Them Something to Eat

“They do not need to go away. You give them something to eat.”

Matthew 14:16

Main activityVocational Training and Empowerment

  • Photography training
  • Graphic design training
  • Practical skills development for young people
  • Preparing trainees for employment and self-employment
  • Praying and sharing the Gospel
04

Project Four

Especially

“Let us do good to everyone, and especially to those who are of the household of faith.”

Galatians 6:10

Main activityEmergency and Household Support

  • Providing house-rent assistance to people in need
  • Distributing food supplies to vulnerable families
  • Providing hygiene and essential household items
  • Praying and sharing the Gospel
05

Project Five

He Died for All

“And he died for all, that those who live might no longer live for themselves but for him who for their sake died and was raised.”

2 Corinthians 5:15

Main activityCompassionate Outreach and Gospel Ministry

  • Providing free shower and personal hygiene services
  • Serving free breakfast
  • Giving soap-making and candle-making vocational training
  • Praying and sharing the Gospel
