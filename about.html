About us

Dignity, education and opportunity.

Hebron Charity works with vulnerable children, schoolgirls, young people and families to meet urgent needs and build lasting opportunity.

Who we are

Hebron Charity is the charitable department of Hebron Faith Church. It operates under the church’s license for charitable activities and runs five projects: feminine hygiene products for schoolgirls, school feeding, vocational training, emergency and household support, and compassionate outreach.
