Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 17 October 2026.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts, does not use cookies, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

The inquiry form

The inquiry form does not send or store anything on this website. When you press “Prepare message”, your browser puts your answers into a WhatsApp message. Nothing is shared until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

We use the details you send only to reply to you and to manage our relationship with you. We do not sell or share them.

The people we serve

We ask for consent before photographing the people we serve, and we do not publish photos in which girls receiving feminine hygiene products can be identified. See safeguarding.
