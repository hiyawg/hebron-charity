Project 01

Feminine Hygiene Products

We produce reusable sanitary pads and give them free of charge to schoolgirls, helping girls stay in school with dignity.

The need

Many schoolgirls from low-income families miss school during their periods because they cannot afford sanitary products. This affects their education, self-esteem, health and future opportunities.

Who we serve and where

  • Girls aged 12 to 19 in primary and secondary schools
  • Orphaned and vulnerable children
  • Girls from low-income households
  • Rural and underserved communities
To be providedThe schools, towns or areas where kits are distributed, and how girls are selected.

What we do

  • Buy fabric, sewing materials and equipment
  • Sew quality reusable sanitary pads, with help from church volunteers
  • Assemble hygiene kits: 4 to 6 reusable pads and a menstrual hygiene information leaflet
  • Run menstrual health education sessions
  • Give the kits free of charge to vulnerable schoolgirls
  • Follow up with girls and schools to collect feedback

Results so far

1,040Female students given feminine hygiene products

Results from May 2025 to September 2026.

To be providedWhether these figures count unique people or times support was given.

Expected outcomes and how we measure them

The figures above are outputs: what we delivered. The changes we work towards are:

  • Better school attendance among girls
  • Better menstrual hygiene and reproductive health knowledge
  • Greater confidence and dignity
  • Lower costs for families
  • Stronger community support for girls’ education

To track progress we record the number of kits produced, the number of girls receiving kits, the number of volunteers trained, the donations received, and feedback from girls and schools.
