Accountability

How we look after your support.

Donors and partners should know how funds are managed, how results are checked and how the people we serve are protected.

Financial transparency and annual reports

Hebron Charity has its own bank account, separate from Hebron Faith Church’s funds. Every payment from it is approved by our Program Manager, Sophonias Melese, and checked by the charity’s finance committee. Each year a financial summary is prepared and reported to Hebron Faith Church management.

Budgets for our projects are published on each project page. See, for example, the Feminine Hygiene Products budget.

Monitoring and evaluation

Our team records what each project delivers at every distribution, and these figures are shared through Hebron Faith Church’s financial reporting. Our impact page shows every figure and how it is counted.

Safeguarding and child protection

Many of the people we serve are children. We follow Ethiopia’s child protection policy, and the Hebron Charity committee is responsible for safeguarding. Volunteers are checked through references before working with children. We do not publish photos in which girls receiving feminine hygiene products can be identified.

Privacy and beneficiary consent

We use written consent forms before photographing the people we serve and before publishing any photo on this website. For children, the form is signed by a parent or guardian. Read our privacy policy for how this website handles your information.

Anti-fraud

Charity funds are held in a dedicated bank account. All spending is approved by the Program Manager and every payment is checked by the finance committee. A yearly financial summary is reported to Hebron Faith Church management.

If you suspect funds have been misused, you can raise it with the Board of Directors of Hebron Faith Church, which oversees the charity from outside the charity team. See complaints below.

Complaints and feedback

There is a comment box at every place where we provide services. You can also call or message us with any question or concern on +251 924 401 884. Our communication committee reads every comment, and we usually reply within 48 hours.

For serious concerns, or if you are not satisfied with our reply, you can take the matter to the Board of Directors of Hebron Faith Church, which is responsible for the charity from outside the charity team.

The Board of Directors can be reached by phone on number to confirm.
